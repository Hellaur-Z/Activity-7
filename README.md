# Activity-7 - Reflection
Creating my resume using ChatGPT and Claude AI was an interesting experience that allowed me to
explore how different artificial intelligence tools can assist in completing a task. Initially, I used ChatGPT to
help me create a professional, modern, and ATS-friendly resume. Although it was helpful in organizing my
information and suggesting professional wording, I encountered difficulties when it came to following my
preferred visual design. Despite giving specific instructions about the layout, colors, and overall
appearance, ChatGPT did not always produce the results I wanted. This became frustrating because I had
a clear idea of how I wanted my resume to look, but the output did not completely match my expectations.
Because of this, I decided to try Claude AI as an alternative. To my surprise, Claude was able to
understand and follow my design preferences more effectively, particularly when it came to the visual
presentation of my resume. It provided an output that was closer to what I had envisioned. This experience
taught me that different AI tools have different strengths and limitations. One tool may be more effective in
generating ideas and organizing information, while another may be better at understanding instructions or
producing a particular output.
Through this activity, I realized the importance of using two different specialized AI tools rather than
depending on only one. Using multiple AI platforms allows us to compare their outputs, identify their
strengths, and choose which tool is most appropriate for a specific task. It also reminds us that AI should
serve as an assistant rather than completely replace our own judgment and decision-making. We still need
to evaluate the results, provide clear instructions, and make necessary adjustments to ensure that the final
output meets our standards.
Overall, this activity taught me to be flexible and resourceful when working with AI. When one tool does not
provide the expected results, exploring other alternatives can lead to better outcomes. More importantly, I
learned that effective AI utilization is not simply about knowing how to use one platform but understanding
how to combine different tools to achieve the best possible results. In the future, I will be more open to
experimenting with different AI technologies and using their individual strengths to improve the quality and
efficiency of my work.
